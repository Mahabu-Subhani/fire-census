**Where fire meets people.** The map can colour every square by population
exposure: squares with fire (at least one detection a year) in the top third of the area for fire activity, split by
whether they are also in the densest third for population (WorldPop 2020).
In Punjab and Haryana, 12.5 million people (20.4 % of the area's population)
live in the high-fire squares, 4.6 million of them in the densely populated
ones. This is exposure, not risk: we have no measure of vulnerability or harm.
