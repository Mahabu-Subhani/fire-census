# Draft for the project page: people near fires, and live NASA fires

For Charulekha to place on the project page. Every number is from the app
(decision 48); re-read it on screen before using it.

**Who lives near the fires?** We joined the record to WorldPop's 2020
population map and gave every ~11 km square a burning level from its fire
detections a year (light 1 to 9, moderate 10 to 49, heavy 50 or more). In
Punjab and Haryana, the raw satellite counts make it look as if the people
living with heavy burning went from 6.3 million a year before 2012 to 34.7
million after, when VIIRS arrived. The harmonized record says 3.0 to 3.2
million: the jump was the new satellite, not more people near fire. Limits:
one population map (2020) for every year; "near" means living in the same
11 km square; the level cut-offs were chosen after looking at the data; it is
exposure, not harm or risk.

**Live layer.** The map can show every VIIRS fire detection from today or
yesterday, straight from NASA GIBS, on top of the 24-year harmonized record.
They are raw detections, not harmonized, and the app is not an alert service.
