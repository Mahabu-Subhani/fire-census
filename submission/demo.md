# Demo: 30-second video (decision 3), target 28 seconds

Over 30 seconds makes the project ineligible. Record from the live app,
https://mahabu-subhani.github.io/fire-census/, not from a mock-up.

## One-sentence pitch: three candidates for the team to choose from

1. For emergency managers watching burning in Córdoba or Punjab, Fire Census
   turns 24 years of NASA satellite hotspots into one calendar where 2005 and
   2025 mean the same, and shows every check it passed and failed.
2. Fire Census tells an emergency manager whether this month's burning is
   unusual, on a 24-year NASA record that no longer jumps when a new satellite
   joins.
3. When MODIS ends, the fire record should not: Fire Census joins MODIS and
   VIIRS into one tested record and a free app that answers "is now unusual?"
   for any area.

**If a take runs over 28 seconds,** cut in this order: "Has the season moved?"
(4 words), then "slowly" moves of the mouse, then "That's the camera, not the
fire." (6 words). Never cut the names, the challenge or the title: the guide
asks for them.

**On-screen captions.** Add the spoken words as English subtitles (YouTube
Studio, Subtitles, type them in). Many judges watch without sound.

**What each line is for (Presentation score).** Lines 1 to 3: the guide's
opening. Line 4: the problem in one image. Line 5: our method, in plain words.
Line 6: what an emergency manager gets. Line 7: proof we are honest, and the
future, because MODIS is ending.

### How to record (Windows, about 20 minutes)

1. Rehearse the words three times with a phone stopwatch.
2. Open the start link in Chrome, full screen (F11), zoom 100 percent, and
   click through every shot once so the charts are loaded and cached.
3. Record with the Xbox Game Bar (Win + Alt + R starts and stops) or OBS. Use
   a headset microphone in a quiet room.
4. Do at least three takes; keep the best. Trim the start and end so the file
   is 30 seconds or less.
5. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, add the subtitles, then open the link in
   a private window, logged out, to confirm it plays.

## Recording checklist

Before recording:

- [ ] Data in the app is the final export; the status sentence matches what the
      project page quotes.
- [ ] Browser zoom 100 percent, window 1280 px wide, no bookmarks bar, no other
      tabs, notifications off.
- [ ] Light or dark theme chosen and kept for the whole take.
- [ ] Mouse moves slowly; each click lands on a visible button.

Content rules (Space Apps guides; breaking one can disqualify):

- [ ] 30 seconds or less in total, including any title card. Aim for 28.
- [ ] English narration, or English subtitles if narrated in another language.
- [ ] No one under 18 visible or audible, including in the background.
- [ ] No NASA logos or NASA branding added to the video.
- [ ] No copyrighted music. Silence or narration only is safest.
- [ ] No AI-generated images or clips. If any is used, a visible watermark
      saying it is AI-generated.

After recording:

- [ ] Check the length in the player: 0:30 or less.
- [ ] Upload to a public host (YouTube unlisted is public to anyone with the
      link, or Google Drive "anyone with the link").
- [ ] Open the link in a private window, logged out: it must play with no login.
- [ ] Paste the link into "Link to demo" and into `submission/project_page.md`.
